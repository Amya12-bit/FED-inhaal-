Meet Our Creators

The Creative Lab represents and supports over 60 independent creators on this platform, bringing together handmade and vintage fashion, jewelry, ceramics, interior, art & care. Our creators are primarily based in the Netherlands, France, and Portugal.

On this platform, every piece is sold directly by the person who crafted or carefully curated it, creating a direct connection between creator and customer. Every purchase directly supports a small business. Meet the people behind the work and discover their stories.
